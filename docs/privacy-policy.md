# Odd Annals Uploader — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Odd Annals Uploader ("the tool") is a private, internal software tool operated by the owner of the
YouTube channel Odd Annals (https://www.youtube.com/@OddAnnals). It is not offered to, and cannot be
used by, anyone else.

## What the tool does
The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, thumbnails, captions and playlist entries to the
Odd Annals channel, and to read public view counts of videos for topic research.

By using YouTube content through this tool you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service
(https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
applies to data processed by YouTube API Services.

## Information collected
- The tool does **not** collect, store or share personal information about any YouTube user or viewer.
- The only account the tool accesses is the channel owner's own account, authorized via Google OAuth.
  The OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and are used only to manage that channel.
- The tool stores the channel's own video IDs, titles and view counts in a private repository to avoid
  repeating topics. Public statistics of other channels are used only at run time and are not stored.
- No cookies are used, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Revoking access
The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at
https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
