# Weekly strategy — 2026-10-12

- **English Shorts Metadata**: English-titled Shorts dominated channel reach. *Windscale 1957* reached **218 views, 5 likes, and 3 comments**, while *The 7000 Year Old Geological Bridge* achieved **88 views and 2 likes** in just 15 hours.
- **Indian Mythological/Geological Hooks**: Ancient history and geological mysteries with strong curiosity hooks (*Geological Bridge* with 88 views) showed significantly faster algorithm uptake compared to generic narrative titles.

- **Tamil Language Content**: Tamil uploads completely stalled, fetching **0 to 3 views** across both Shorts (*Aleya* at 0 views, *Ram Setu* at 3 views) and Long-form (*Dwarka* at 1 view).

- **Premature Compilations & Long-Form**: *Odd Annals Weekly* compilation (**1 view**) and *Dwarka EP 1* (**2 views**) suffered from zero initial push due to lack of an established subscriber base to sustain long-form watch time.

#### Concrete Changes for Next Week
1. **Pause Tamil Content**: Stop Tamil localization immediately to avoid diluting channel authority; focus strictly on Hindi narration with Hinglish/English searchable titles.
2. **Shift Short Topic Selection**: Replace niche European history with highly recognizable Indian mysteries, ancient monuments, and lost civilizations.
3. **Short-to-Long Funneling**: Use high-performing English/Hinglish Shorts as teaser hooks driving directly to long-form episodes using YouTube's Related Video link feature.
4. **Scrap Weekly Compilations**: Discontinue weekly compilation long-form videos until reaching monetization thresholds.
