Odd Annals Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Odd Annals Uploader ("the tool") is a private, internal software tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels Odd Annals and its regional-language channels (for example Odd Annals Tamil). It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.

What the tool does

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, thumbnails, captions, playlist entries and comments to the channel owner's own channels, and to read view counts and analytics of those channels and public statistics of other videos for topic research. By using YouTube content through this tool you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by YouTube API Services.

Information collected

  • The tool does not collect, store or share personal information about any YouTube user or viewer.
  • The only accounts the tool accesses are the channel owner's own channels, authorised via Google OAuth. OAuth credentials are kept as encrypted secrets and used only to manage those channels.
  • The tool stores the channels' own video IDs, titles and view statistics to plan topics. Public statistics of other channels are used only at run time and are not stored.
  • When answering viewer questions, at most a commenter's first name (already public on YouTube) may be mentioned in a video. No other comment data is kept.
  • No cookies are used and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel via youtube.com/@oddannals.
